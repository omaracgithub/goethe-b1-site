Frequently asked questions

What is the Goethe-Zertifikat B1?

The Goethe-Zertifikat B1 certifies intermediate German. It proves you can handle most situations while travelling, discuss familiar topics, and describe experiences and opinions. It's the standard language requirement for German citizenship and is accepted by many employers and Studienkolleg programs.

How long is the Goethe B1 exam?

The written portion takes about 2 hours and 15 minutes: reading (65 min), listening (40 min), and writing (60 min). The speaking module adds approximately 15 minutes, usually conducted in pairs. Total exam time is around 2.5 hours.

What score do I need to pass?

You need at least 60 out of 100 points. Each module (Lesen, Hören, Schreiben, Sprechen) is graded separately, and you must achieve at least 60% in each. You can retake individual failed modules.

How does the AI writing review work?

Submit your written response and receive instant scoring against official B1 criteria. The AI evaluates task completion, coherence, grammatical accuracy, and vocabulary range — giving you specific, actionable feedback to improve.
